# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Sea Battle So Called (or Mobotan) is a board game where each player places a hidden fleet on a 10×10 grid and takes turns firing shots at the opponent's grid. Players receive feedback on each shot — Miss, Hit, or Sunk — and earn an extra turn for every successful hit or sunk result. The match ends when one player sinks all 20 segments of the opponent's fleet. The game follows the classic ruleset, including the alphanumeric coordinate system (digits 1–10 for rows, letters A–J for columns) and the adjacency rule requiring at least one empty cell between all ships in all eight directions. In addition to the classic two-player local mode, the game supports a single-player mode where a human plays against an AI opponent, and an online multiplayer mode where two human players compete over the Internet — either by direct invite or through a matchmaking queue.

## Glossary

- **Game**: The Sea Battle application managing the full match lifecycle.
- **Board**: A 10×10 grid belonging to one player, containing that player's fleet and tracking incoming shots.
- **Cell**: A single position on the Board identified by a column letter and a row number (e.g., А1, J10).
- **Fleet**: The complete set of 10 ships a player must place before the match begins.
- **Ship**: A vessel occupying one or more consecutive Cells in a horizontal or vertical line.
- **Battleship**: A Ship occupying 4 Cells. One per Fleet.
- **Cruiser**: A Ship occupying 3 Cells. Two per Fleet.
- **Destroyer**: A Ship occupying 2 Cells. Three per Fleet.
- **Patrol_Boat**: A Ship occupying 1 Cell. Four per Fleet.
- **Segment**: A single Cell occupied by a Ship. Each Fleet contains 20 Segments total.
- **Buffer_Zone**: The ring of empty Cells surrounding a Ship in all eight directions (orthogonal and diagonal).
- **Placement_Engine**: The subsystem responsible for validating and recording Ship placements on a Board.
- **Shot_Engine**: The subsystem responsible for processing shots, updating Cell statuses, and evaluating turn transitions.
- **Turn_Manager**: The subsystem responsible for controlling which player fires next and applying extra-turn logic.
- **Victory_Detector**: The subsystem responsible for detecting when all 20 Segments of a Fleet have been sunk.
- **Coordinate_System**: The Russian alphanumeric labeling scheme — columns A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J (left to right) and rows 1–10 (top to bottom).
- **Miss**: Shot result indicating the targeted Cell contains no Ship Segment.
- **Hit**: Shot result indicating the targeted Cell contains a Ship Segment that has not yet caused the Ship to be fully sunk.
- **Sunk**: Shot result indicating the targeted Cell contains the last unsunk Segment of a Ship, causing the Ship to be fully destroyed.
- **Adjacency_Rule**: The constraint that no two Ships may occupy Cells that are orthogonally or diagonally adjacent.
- **AI_Opponent**: The computer-controlled player that automatically places its Fleet and autonomously selects shot coordinates during a single-player match.
- **Online_Match**: A match between two human players conducted over a network connection, where game state is kept in sync between both clients.
- **Session**: A server-side record representing an Online_Match, identified by a unique Session identifier and holding the current state of both Boards and the turn order.
- **Invite_Code**: A short alphanumeric token generated by the Game that a player can share with a specific person to join their Online_Match.
- **Matchmaking**: The process by which the Game pairs two players who are each waiting for a random opponent into a new Online_Match.
- **Matchmaking_Queue**: The ordered list of players waiting to be paired via Matchmaking.
- **Network_Manager**: The subsystem responsible for transmitting game events between clients and the server during an Online_Match and for detecting disconnections.
- **User_Account**: A persistent record associated with a registered player, containing credentials and profile data, identified by a unique immutable account identifier.
- **Display_Name**: The human-readable name associated with a User_Account that is visible to other players during matches and in the matchmaking interface.
- **Profile_Icon**: A visual avatar selected from the Icon_Library and associated with a User_Account to represent the player in the game interface.
- **Icon_Library**: The predefined, system-managed collection of selectable Profile_Icons available to all players; players may not upload custom images.
- **Authentication**: The process by which the Game verifies a player's identity by validating their email address and password against the stored User_Account credentials.
- **Account_Manager**: The subsystem responsible for registration, authentication, profile updates, and account deletion.
- **Email_Verifier**: The subsystem responsible for validating email address format and confirming ownership of an email address via a verification message.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Board and Coordinate System

**User Story:** As a player, I want the game board to use the Russian alphanumeric coordinate system, so that I can identify cells using familiar notation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Board SHALL consist of exactly 100 Cells arranged in a 10-column by 10-row grid.
2. THE Coordinate_System SHALL label columns with the letters A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J from left to right.
3. THE Coordinate_System SHALL label rows with the integers 1 through 10 from top to bottom.
4. THE Board SHALL uniquely identify each Cell by the combination of its column letter and row number (e.g., А1, J10).
5. IF a coordinate supplied to any subsystem does not correspond to a valid column letter and row number pair, THEN THE Game SHALL reject the coordinate and return a descriptive error message.

---

### Requirement 2: Fleet Composition

**User Story:** As a player, I want the game to enforce the standard fleet composition, so that every match uses the correct number and size of ships.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Fleet SHALL contain exactly 1 Battleship (4 Segments), 2 Cruisers (3 Segments each), 3 Destroyers (2 Segments each), and 4 Patrol_Boats (1 Segment each).
2. THE Fleet SHALL contain exactly 10 Ships and exactly 20 Segments in total.
3. WHEN a player attempts to add a Ship type that would exceed the allowed count for that type, THE Placement_Engine SHALL reject the placement and return a descriptive error message.

---

### Requirement 3: Ship Placement — Orientation and Bounds

**User Story:** As a player, I want ships to be placed only horizontally or vertically within the grid, so that placement follows the rules of the game.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Placement_Engine SHALL accept Ship placements only in horizontal or vertical orientation.
2. WHEN a Ship is placed horizontally, THE Placement_Engine SHALL require all of the Ship's Cells to share the same row number.
3. WHEN a Ship is placed vertically, THE Placement_Engine SHALL require all of the Ship's Cells to share the same column letter.
4. IF any Cell of a Ship would fall outside the 10×10 Board boundary, THEN THE Placement_Engine SHALL reject the placement and return a descriptive error message.

---

### Requirement 4: Ship Placement — Adjacency Rule

**User Story:** As a player, I want the system to block ship placement if ships touch each other at the sides or corners, so that the adjacency rule is enforced.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Placement_Engine SHALL enforce the Adjacency_Rule for every Ship placement.
2. WHEN a Ship is placed, THE Placement_Engine SHALL check all eight neighbors (orthogonal and diagonal) of every Cell the Ship occupies.
3. IF any neighbor Cell of the Ship being placed is already occupied by another Ship, THEN THE Placement_Engine SHALL reject the placement and return a descriptive error message.
4. IF any neighbor Cell of the Ship being placed falls within the Buffer_Zone of an already-placed Ship, THEN THE Placement_Engine SHALL reject the placement and return a descriptive error message.

---

### Requirement 5: Match Start Condition

**User Story:** As a player, I want the match to begin only after both players have placed all their ships, so that neither player starts at a disadvantage.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Game SHALL require both players to complete Fleet placement before the first shot may be fired.
2. WHEN a player has placed all 10 Ships of their Fleet, THE Placement_Engine SHALL mark that player's Fleet as ready.
3. WHILE at least one player's Fleet is not ready, THE Game SHALL prevent any shot from being fired.
4. WHEN both players' Fleets are ready, THE Game SHALL transition to the shooting phase and assign the first turn to a player.

---

### Requirement 6: Shot Processing and Cell Status

**User Story:** As a player, I want to fire shots at the opponent's grid and receive accurate feedback, so that I can deduce the location of enemy ships.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a player fires a shot at a Cell that contains no Ship Segment, THE Shot_Engine SHALL record the Cell status as Miss.
2. WHEN a player fires a shot at a Cell that contains a Ship Segment and the Ship still has at least one unsunk Segment remaining after the shot, THE Shot_Engine SHALL record the Cell status as Hit.
3. WHEN a player fires a shot at a Cell that contains the last unsunk Segment of a Ship, THE Shot_Engine SHALL record the Cell status as Sunk and mark the Ship as fully destroyed.
4. THE Shot_Engine SHALL expose the shot result (Miss, Hit, Sunk) to both players after each shot.
5. IF a player attempts to fire a shot at a Cell that has already been shot, THEN THE Shot_Engine SHALL reject the shot and return a descriptive error message without consuming the player's turn.

---

### Requirement 7: Turn Management and Extra-Turn Rule

**User Story:** As a player, I want to receive an extra turn immediately after a successful Hit or Sunk result, so that skilled shooting is rewarded.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Shot_Engine returns a result of Miss, THE Turn_Manager SHALL transfer the active turn to the other player.
2. WHEN the Shot_Engine returns a result of Hit, THE Turn_Manager SHALL grant the current player an additional turn immediately.
3. WHEN the Shot_Engine returns a result of Sunk, THE Turn_Manager SHALL grant the current player an additional turn immediately.
4. THE Turn_Manager SHALL continue granting additional turns to the current player for each consecutive Hit or Sunk result until the player fires a shot that results in Miss.

---

### Requirement 8: Buffer Zone Auto-Mark on Sunk

**User Story:** As a player, I want the grid to automatically mark the buffer zone around a sunk ship as Missed, so that I can focus on cells that may still contain ships.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Ship is marked as fully destroyed (Sunk), THE Shot_Engine SHALL automatically mark all Buffer_Zone Cells surrounding that Ship as Miss, provided those Cells have not already been shot.
2. THE Shot_Engine SHALL apply the auto-mark to all eight neighbors (orthogonal and diagonal) of every Cell the destroyed Ship occupies.
3. THE Shot_Engine SHALL skip auto-marking any Buffer_Zone Cell that already has a recorded shot status.
4. THE Shot_Engine SHALL make the auto-marked Cells visible to both players on the opponent's grid view.

---

### Requirement 9: Victory Condition

**User Story:** As a player, I want the match to end immediately when all enemy ship segments are sunk, so that the winner is determined correctly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Victory_Detector SHALL track the total number of Segments sunk in each player's Fleet.
2. WHEN the total number of sunk Segments in a player's Fleet reaches 20, THE Victory_Detector SHALL declare the opposing player as the winner and end the match immediately.
3. WHEN the match ends, THE Game SHALL prevent any further shots from being fired.
4. THE Game SHALL display the final result, identifying the winning player.

---

### Requirement 10: Data Model Integrity

**User Story:** As a developer, I want the game's data model to accurately track cell and ship state, so that all game logic operates on consistent and reliable data.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Game SHALL represent each Cell with at minimum: column identifier, row identifier, and shot status (unshot, Miss, Hit, or Sunk).
2. THE Game SHALL represent each Ship with at minimum: type, size, list of occupied Cell coordinates, count of hit Segments, and a flag indicating whether the Ship is fully sunk.
3. WHEN a Segment is hit, THE Game SHALL increment the hit Segment count for the corresponding Ship.
4. WHEN the hit Segment count of a Ship equals the Ship's size, THE Game SHALL set the Ship's sunk flag to true.
5. THE Game SHALL maintain separate Board instances for each player so that one player's data does not affect the other's.

---

### Requirement 11: Round-Trip Coordinate Serialization

**User Story:** As a developer, I want coordinates to serialize and deserialize correctly, so that game state can be saved, transmitted, and restored without data loss.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Coordinate_System SHALL provide a serializer that converts a Cell coordinate (column letter, row number) to a canonical string representation (e.g., "G7").
2. THE Coordinate_System SHALL provide a parser that converts a canonical string representation back to a Cell coordinate (column letter, row number).
3. IF an invalid string is supplied to the parser, THEN THE Coordinate_System SHALL return a descriptive error without producing a Cell coordinate.
4. FOR ALL valid Cell coordinates, parsing the serialized form of the coordinate SHALL produce a coordinate equal to the original (round-trip property).

---

### Requirement 12: Single-Player vs AI Mode

**User Story:** As a player, I want to play a game against an AI opponent, so that I can enjoy Sea Battle without needing a second human player.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Game SHALL provide a single-player mode in which a human player competes against an AI_Opponent.
2. WHEN a single-player match is started, THE AI_Opponent SHALL automatically place its complete Fleet on its Board before the shooting phase begins, satisfying all placement rules defined in Requirements 3 and 4.
3. WHEN it is the AI_Opponent's turn to fire, THE AI_Opponent SHALL autonomously select a valid, previously unshot Cell and submit a shot without requiring human input.
4. THE AI_Opponent SHALL follow all turn rules defined in Requirement 7, taking additional turns on Hit or Sunk results and yielding the turn on a Miss result.
5. THE AI_Opponent SHALL follow all game rules defined in Requirements 1 through 11 as a valid opponent.
6. WHEN the Victory_Detector declares a winner in a single-player match, THE Game SHALL identify whether the human player or the AI_Opponent has won and display the final result.

---

### Requirement 13: Online Multiplayer — Match Setup via Invite

**User Story:** As a player, I want to invite a specific person to play against me over the Internet, so that I can start an online match with a friend or chosen opponent.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Game SHALL provide an online multiplayer mode in which two human players compete over a network connection via an Online_Match.
2. WHEN a player chooses to invite a specific opponent, THE Game SHALL generate a unique Invite_Code and present it to the inviting player.
3. WHEN a second player enters a valid Invite_Code, THE Game SHALL associate that player with the corresponding Session and transition both players to the Fleet placement phase.
4. IF an Invite_Code does not correspond to any open Session, THEN THE Game SHALL reject the code and return a descriptive error message to the player who entered it.
5. WHEN both players in an Online_Match have completed Fleet placement, THE Network_Manager SHALL synchronize the start of the shooting phase for both clients simultaneously.
6. WHEN a player fires a shot in an Online_Match, THE Network_Manager SHALL transmit the shot and its result to both clients so that both Boards remain in sync.
7. THE Game SHALL enforce turn order over the network so that only the player whose turn it is may fire a shot at any given time.

---

### Requirement 14: Online Multiplayer — Matchmaking and Disconnection Handling

**User Story:** As a player, I want to be paired with a random available opponent and have the game handle disconnections gracefully, so that I can start an online match quickly and have a consistent experience if the connection is interrupted.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Game SHALL provide a matchmaking option that places a player into the Matchmaking_Queue to be paired with a random available opponent.
2. WHEN two players are present in the Matchmaking_Queue, THE Game SHALL pair them into a new Online_Match and remove both players from the Matchmaking_Queue.
3. WHILE a player is waiting in the Matchmaking_Queue, THE Game SHALL display a status indicator informing the player that a match is being sought.
4. WHEN a player in the Matchmaking_Queue cancels the search, THE Game SHALL remove that player from the Matchmaking_Queue and return them to the main menu.
5. WHEN the Network_Manager detects that a player has disconnected during an Online_Match, THE Game SHALL notify the remaining player that the opponent has disconnected.
6. IF a disconnected player does not reconnect within a defined timeout period, THEN THE Game SHALL end the Online_Match and declare the remaining connected player as the winner.
7. WHEN a disconnected player reconnects to their Session within the timeout period, THE Network_Manager SHALL restore the current Board state for both players and resume the match from the point of disconnection.

---

### Requirement 15: User Registration

**User Story:** As a new player, I want to register an account with my email address, a display name, a profile icon, and a password, so that I can access the game and be identified to other players.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Account_Manager SHALL require every player to hold a registered User_Account before starting or joining any match.
2. WHEN a player submits a registration request, THE Account_Manager SHALL require the request to include an email address, a Display_Name, a Profile_Icon selection, and a password.
3. WHEN a registration request is received, THE Email_Verifier SHALL validate that the supplied email address conforms to standard email address format (local-part@domain).
4. IF the supplied email address does not conform to standard email address format, THEN THE Account_Manager SHALL reject the registration and return a descriptive error message.
5. WHEN a registration request is received, THE Account_Manager SHALL verify that the supplied email address is not already associated with an existing User_Account.
6. IF the supplied email address is already associated with an existing User_Account, THEN THE Account_Manager SHALL reject the registration and return a descriptive error message.
7. WHEN a registration request is received, THE Account_Manager SHALL verify that the supplied Profile_Icon is a member of the Icon_Library.
8. IF the supplied Profile_Icon is not a member of the Icon_Library, THEN THE Account_Manager SHALL reject the registration and return a descriptive error message.
9. THE Account_Manager SHALL assign each new User_Account a unique, immutable account identifier at the time of creation.
10. WHEN all registration fields are valid and the email address is unique, THE Account_Manager SHALL create the User_Account and confirm successful registration to the player.
11. WHEN a User_Account is successfully created, THE Account_Manager SHALL permit the player to authenticate using the registered email address and password.

---

### Requirement 16: User Profile Update

**User Story:** As a registered player, I want to update my display name, profile icon, password, or email address, so that I can keep my profile current.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE a player is authenticated, THE Account_Manager SHALL permit the player to update their Display_Name, Profile_Icon, password, or email address.
2. WHEN a player submits a Display_Name update, THE Account_Manager SHALL replace the stored Display_Name with the new value and confirm the change to the player.
3. WHEN a player submits a Profile_Icon update, THE Account_Manager SHALL verify that the new Profile_Icon is a member of the Icon_Library.
4. IF the supplied Profile_Icon is not a member of the Icon_Library, THEN THE Account_Manager SHALL reject the update and return a descriptive error message.
5. WHEN a player submits a password update, THE Account_Manager SHALL require the player to supply their current password for verification before accepting the new password.
6. IF the supplied current password does not match the stored credential, THEN THE Account_Manager SHALL reject the password update and return a descriptive error message.
7. WHEN the current password is verified, THE Account_Manager SHALL replace the stored password with the new password and confirm the change to the player.
8. WHEN a player submits an email address update, THE Email_Verifier SHALL validate that the new email address conforms to standard email address format.
9. IF the new email address does not conform to standard email address format, THEN THE Account_Manager SHALL reject the update and return a descriptive error message.
10. WHEN a valid new email address is submitted, THE Account_Manager SHALL send a verification message to the new email address and mark the address as unverified until the player confirms ownership.
11. WHILE the new email address is unverified, THE Account_Manager SHALL retain the previous email address as the active credential for Authentication.
12. THE Account_Manager SHALL prevent any update from modifying the unique account identifier assigned at registration.

---

### Requirement 17: User Profile Deletion

**User Story:** As a registered player, I want to permanently delete my account, so that my personal data is removed from the system when I no longer wish to use the game.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE a player is authenticated, THE Account_Manager SHALL permit the player to request permanent deletion of their own User_Account.
2. WHEN a deletion request is received, THE Account_Manager SHALL present an explicit confirmation prompt informing the player that the action is permanent and irreversible before proceeding.
3. IF the player does not confirm the deletion prompt, THEN THE Account_Manager SHALL cancel the deletion and leave the User_Account unchanged.
4. WHEN the player confirms the deletion prompt, THE Account_Manager SHALL permanently remove the User_Account record and all associated personal data from the system.
5. WHEN a User_Account is deleted, THE Account_Manager SHALL immediately invalidate all active Authentication sessions associated with that account.
6. WHEN a User_Account is deleted while the player is a participant in one or more in-progress Online_Matches, THE Game SHALL end each affected Online_Match, notify the opponent that their opponent's account has been removed, and award the opponent a win for each such match.
7. WHEN a deleted User_Account's player attempts to authenticate after deletion, THE Account_Manager SHALL reject the Authentication attempt and return a descriptive error message.
